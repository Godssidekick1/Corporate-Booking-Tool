# Third-party data

## GeoNames

The country, state and city lists (the `countries`, `regions` and `cities`
reference tables, loaded by `scripts/load-cities.mjs` and the places
migration) come from GeoNames, https://www.geonames.org, licensed under
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The data is used as published, filtered to populated places of 500 or more
people.
